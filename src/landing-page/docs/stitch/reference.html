Operational Clarity

Frequently asked questions

Institutional policies covering pilot coverage, verification procedures, and release distributions.

What is AISLEY and how does archipelagic commerce work?

AISLEY is a Philippine commerce network connecting verified local sellers, regional sorting facilities, and delivery riders. Rather than functioning as an unmonitored marketplace or classifieds board, it coordinates physical hub handovers and Cash on Delivery (COD) milestones under structured chain-of-custody protocols.

What are the current pilot coverage areas?

Early access deployment currently covers designated municipal hubs in Metro Manila (including Mandaluyong, Pasig, Quezon City, and Taguig) along with select Northern Luzon agricultural supply corridors connecting Benguet and Baguio producers with metropolitan buyers.

How does Admin approval work for sellers and logistics hubs?

To prevent fraud and maintain delivery reliability, independent merchants must complete seller verification and catalog review before receiving access to seller.aisley.app. Logistics hub coordinators and courier affiliates similarly require operational clearance prior to handling parcel dispatch queues.

Where can users download the Android APKs?

Standalone APK packages for both the customer client and rider operational build are published directly to GitHub Releases under github.com/ApueXI/aisley-app-customer and github.com/ApueXI/aisley-app-rider. Shoppers may also browse and checkout entirely through modern mobile web browsers without installing native software.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) milestone tracking protect both parties?

Buyers inspect parcel tamper seals and verify that the package has successfully cleared all regional sorting milestones before releasing cash to the rider. In turn, sellers benefit from verified digital proof of delivery and an unbroken transit log before funds are reconciled into their account.
