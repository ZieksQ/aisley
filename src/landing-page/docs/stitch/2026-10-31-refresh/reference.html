Frequently Asked Questions

Questions & answers

Important details on platform access, marketplace coverage, and operations.

What is AISLEY?
AISLEY is a Philippine multi-vendor marketplace connecting independent shops directly with buyers. It supports Cash on Delivery checkout and tracks orders across verified merchant preparation and delivery phases.
Is AISLEY available everywhere?
AISLEY is currently in early access. Product availability and specific delivery options depend strictly on participating shops and their active operational coverage. Buyers should review individual item availability and checkout options for their address.
How does Admin approval work for sellers and logistics hubs?
Both Seller and Logistics hub portal accounts require manual verification and approval by AISLEY Platform Administrators before merchant or hub tools can be accessed. Individual couriers are subsequently approved and onboarded directly by their associated Logistics organization.
Where can I get mobile apps?
Mobile application packages for both Buyers and affiliated Riders are released directly on GitHub via official public repositories (ApueXI/aisley-app-customer and ApueXI/aisley-app-rider). Visitors should inspect the release notes and package assets directly before installing.
Does tracking show a live courier location?
No. Order tracking shows recorded shipment milestones (such as parcel packed, waybill printed, hub handoff, and out for delivery). Continuous real-time GPS courier coordinates are not promised or provided.
