# Development database seeding

`php artisan db:seed` creates or completes the local demo accounts and catalog. The seeders preserve existing passwords, account states, shop settings, product edits, inventory balances and movement history, provider choices, tariff/rate-card histories, sorting plans, courier review decisions, and manually cleared coordinates. They add missing fixture records and print a warning when an existing configuration needs operator attention.

Set `INITIAL_ADMIN_EMAIL` and `INITIAL_ADMIN_PASSWORD`, `INITIAL_SELLER_EMAIL` and `INITIAL_SELLER_PASSWORD`, `INITIAL_LOGISTICS_EMAIL` and `INITIAL_LOGISTICS_PASSWORD`, and `APPROVED_CUSTOMER_EMAIL` and `APPROVED_CUSTOMER_PASSWORD` in `src/api/.env` to create the primary accounts. The initial Logistics organization and hub, Seller shop, and customer delivery address use the other `INITIAL_*` and `APPROVED_CUSTOMER_*` values in `src/api/.env.example`. Courier fixtures additionally require `INITIAL_COURIER_EMAIL` and `INITIAL_COURIER_PASSWORD`. Existing records keep their password and status when seeding again.

Generic Customer, Seller, Logistics, and Courier accounts and the regional Luzon demo organizations are development fixtures. The explicitly configured initial accounts may be seeded in production, but generic accounts, the catalog, and checkout demonstration settings are skipped there. Policy-consent records and transactional activity are never fabricated.

The checkout fixture runs after `ProductSeeder`. When no platform tariff or commission policy history exists, it creates a zero-surcharge platform tariff and zero-percent Seller and Logistics policies. It records tariff acceptance for the configured initial Logistics organization and publishes a rate card for first mile and last mile. The default service bases are ₱30 and ₱40. Each leg includes up to 1 kg, then adds ₱5 per additional 500 g. The card inherits parcel limits from the active platform tariff. A local standard sorting plan covers the initial hub's postal code, and the configured Customer receives a matching shipping address only if they have no addresses.

Checkout setup is additive. Existing tariff and commission histories are not replaced. Existing rate cards are not archived or superseded. A disabled Seller provider, inactive coverage, or operator sorting plan stays as configured; the seeder reports what must be reviewed. To obtain a local route, the initial Seller and Shop must be active with a complete default pickup address, the initial Logistics account must be active with a hub address, and the active main Shop Category must match the demo store.

Run `php artisan migrate --seed` to apply pending migrations and seed an existing local database. Run `php artisan db:seed` to apply the fixtures again. `php artisan migrate:fresh --seed` deletes all existing data and belongs only on a disposable local or test database.
