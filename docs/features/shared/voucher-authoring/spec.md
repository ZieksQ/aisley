# Voucher authoring

## Scope and terms

Admin authors platform-funded App merchandise/shipping vouchers. Seller authors merchandise vouchers for the authenticated Seller's Shop, funded through reduced merchandise proceeds before commission. All eligible items only in v1; Product/Category/Customer authoring, campaigns and scheduled replacement offers remain deferred. Existing targeted or Seller-shipping definitions remain readable and usable by checkout, but cannot be revised or duplicated through authoring.

Platform distribution is `automatic` or `claim_required`, defaulting to claim-required for new drafts. Seller terms always use claim-required without a configurable field. Include distribution in working terms, publication confirmation, details, new snapshots and duplication; historical version JSON stays immutable. Duplicate to change distribution after publication.

Terms are complete on every draft save: PHP, COD, positive fixed amount or percentage (at most 100), optional positive savings cap, minimum spend (default zero), UTC start/end (end strictly after start), optional positive total limit, positive per-Customer limit (default one), plain-text terms and a required customer-visible name (1–120 trimmed plain-text characters). UI displays Asia/Manila. Codes are globally unique uppercase letters/digits/hyphens, generated by default and editable until first publication. Names identify vouchers in Customer selection; codes remain display identifiers and Customers continue selecting UUIDs. Existing names fall back to their codes; published version JSON and existing Order snapshots remain intact. Names can change through working draft publication.

## Lifecycle and concurrency

Drafts are invisible to Customer discovery and unusable in checkout. First publication may start now or later; server time determines Scheduled/Active/Expired without a job. Separate working versions preserve live terms until replacement publication. Replacement takes effect immediately under the same UUID/code and preserves cumulative usage; issuer, Shop, benefit, code and distribution are fixed after first publication. Replacement requires both old and proposed start to have arrived and proposed end to be future; duplicate for an independent future offer or changed benefit. Total limit cannot be below committed usage.

Pause/resume changes availability only. Replacement of paused live terms remains paused. End is permanent, including for drafts; ended vouchers cannot be saved/published/resumed. Duplication produces an independent complete draft, new UUID/code and zero usage. Discarding a replacement restores the published view; an initial draft has no published fallback and can only be ended. No hard deletion, cancellation restoration or automatic reissue.

Each mutation requires UUID Idempotency-Key and integer revision (zero for create). Receipts are actor/key scoped; same normalized payload/action/target replays the original private response, changed payload or stale revision conflicts. Serialize receipt reservation then Voucher row locking; checkout uses that same row. Published version and availability revision participate in quote hashing, including same-second mutations. Existing Orders retain their original snapshots. Draft/published versions and append-only action events preserve evidence. Backfill creates published baselines without altering existing IDs, terms, counters, availability or Order snapshots.

## APIs

Role prefixes `/api/v1/admin/vouchers` and `/api/v1/seller/vouchers`: GET list (search/status/benefit/page, 15 rows), POST complete draft, GET `/{voucher}`, PUT `/{voucher}/draft`, POST `/{voucher}/{discard|publish|duplicate|pause|resume|end}`, GET `/{voucher}/{versions|actions|redemptions}` (paginated). Reject ownership, issuer, counters, lifecycle and arbitrary targeting fields. Seller rejects shipping; foreign vouchers return 404. Sanctum, active-role/approval and consent apply throughout. Admin requires vouchers.view for reads and both vouchers.view/vouchers.manage for mutations. No Customer identity appears in reports or legacy version terms: Customer targeting IDs remain stored but are redacted from authoring responses, with `eligibility_scope = legacy_targeted` retaining the restriction label. Preserve exact legacy published display codes; normalize new codes only.

Status precedence: Ended, Draft, Paused, Expired, Scheduled, Exhausted, Active. Report cumulative committed redemptions, remaining capacity (null for unlimited), Customer savings and Order reference/date/version/amount including cancelled Orders. Savings are not settled expenses or profit.

## Web experience

Role-owned `/vouchers`, `/vouchers/new`, `/vouchers/:id`; Admin under Platform with permissions, Seller under Shop. Reuse shared presentation and @aisley/ui with existing themes, typography, simple borders and 6–8px radii. Lists provide search/status/benefit filters and pagination. Labeled editor sections: details (name/code), benefit, conditions, schedule, limits and terms. Savings cap (PHP, optional) belongs in Conditions; both Saving/Percentage and Savings cap have a circle-exclamation tooltip beside the label, usable by hover, focus, tap and Escape. Details show live terms, explicit pending differences, totals, versions/actions/redemptions and pagination. Confirm publish with exact terms/Manila schedule/funding, pause/end and unsaved discard. Preserve safe input on recoverable errors and the exact uncertain request for retry; block dependent actions until uncertainty resolves. Clear scoped state on account/authorization loss. Check 390/768/1440 widths, light/dark, keyboard/focus, loading/empty/error and read-only permissions.

## Funding and checkout invariants

Preserve integer-cent rounding, caps and eligible bases. One discount and one shipping voucher combine by default per Shop Order, including older definitions with empty stacking policies. Reject multiple vouchers of the same benefit per Shop and allow at most one App voucher of each benefit per checkout, each with an explicit Shop target. Legacy authoring `stacking` booleans are accepted but cannot disable this rule; no stacking creation control exists. Seller discount reduces commission basis and flows once through settlement. Platform discounts/shipping preserve Seller/Logistics proceeds; existing ledger and funding snapshots remain authoritative.

## Marketplace references

Its name is private; AISLEY names are customer-visible by explicit product requirement. [Lazada shipping guidance](https://www.lazada.com.ph/blog/free-shipping-home-appliances-guide/) shows voucher selection at checkout, minimum spend and delivery limits. These inform familiar field grouping; AISLEY’s requested one-per-benefit rule and role/funding boundaries are authoritative, rather than a claim that both marketplaces have identical stacking rules.

Customer discovery/collection/wallet follows the [Customer voucher contract](../../customer/voucher-usage/spec.md). Existing platform projections backfill to automatic; existing Shop offers require collection for future orders. Collection never reserves redemption capacity or alters historical Orders.
